Lesson 01 Matemwe Secondary School · first class Shule ya Sekondari Matemwe · somo la kwanza 马特姆韦中学 · 第一课

You are not here to study AI. You are here to teach with it — and to walk out today with a lesson you will actually give this week. Hamko hapa kujifunza AI. Mko hapa kufundisha kwa kutumia AI — na kuondoka leo na somo mtakalofundisha wiki hii. 你们不是来"学AI"的，是来用AI教书的——今天离开时，每人带走一节本周就要上的课。

The one thing · Lengo moja · 唯一目标

At 15:00 every teacher leaves with three things Saa tisa kila mwalimu anaondoka na vitu vitatu 15:00，每位教师带走三样东西

Output · Matokeo · 成果

  • A finished lesson plan for a real class on your own timetable. Andalio kamili la somo halisi lililopo katika ratiba yako. 一份完整教案，对应你课表上的真实一课。
  • A one-page teaching page saved on the laptop, opening without internet. Ukurasa mmoja wa kufundishia uliohifadhiwa kwenye kompyuta, unaofunguka bila intaneti. 一张存在电脑里的教学网页，断网也能打开。
  • A date, written down, when you will teach it. Tarehe, iliyoandikwa, utakayofundisha somo hilo. 一个写下来的日期——你哪天上这节课。

Not today · Si leo · 今天不做

  • No theory of artificial intelligence. No history, no vocabulary test. Hakuna nadharia ya akili bandia. Hakuna historia, hakuna mtihani wa msamiati. 不讲人工智能理论、历史，不考名词。
  • No slides you will never use again. Hakuna slaidi ambazo hamtazitumia tena. 不做用一次就扔的幻灯片。
  • No pretending. If the machine is wrong, we say so out loud. Hakuna kujifanya. Mashine ikikosea, tunasema wazi. 不装懂。机器错了，就当场说出来。

Phase 01 · Naming

Who is in the room Nani yupo darasani 这间屋子里的人是谁

Trainer · Mkufunzi · 培训者

  • Sign the register. Hand out the one-page pre-survey (8 items) and collect it before Phase 02. Saini rejista. Toa dodoso la ukurasa mmoja (maswali 8) na likusanye kabla ya Awamu ya 02. 签到。发前测问卷（一页8题），第二阶段开始前收回。
  • Name each teacher by subject and form, out loud: "You teach Form 2 Biology." Mtaje kila mwalimu kwa somo na kidato, kwa sauti: "Wewe unafundisha Biolojia Kidato cha Pili." 当众点名到学科与年级："你教二年级生物。"

Teacher · Mwalimu · 教师

  • Say the class you will teach next week and the exact topic from the syllabus. Taja darasa utakalofundisha wiki ijayo na mada kamili kutoka mtaala. 说出下周要上的班级，以及大纲里的具体课题。
  • Write that topic at the top of your notebook. Everything today is built on it. Andika mada hiyo juu ya daftari lako. Kila kitu cha leo kinajengwa juu yake. 把课题写在笔记本第一行。今天所有内容都围绕它。

Phase 02 · Live demonstration

What it does well, what it gets wrong Inachofanya vizuri, na inachokosea 它擅长什么，它会错在哪

Strong · Ina nguvu · 强

  • Drafting: lesson plans, exercises, exam questions with answer keys, notes at two difficulty levels. Kuandaa: maandalio, mazoezi, maswali ya mtihani na majibu, madokezo ya viwango viwili. 起草：教案、练习、带答案的考题、两个难度层次的讲义。
  • Explaining one idea five different ways until one fits your class. Kueleza wazo moja kwa njia tano tofauti hadi mojawapo iwafae wanafunzi wako. 把同一个概念换五种讲法，直到有一种适合你的班。
  • Translating between English, Kiswahili and simple language for slow readers. Kutafsiri kati ya Kiingereza, Kiswahili na lugha rahisi kwa wasomaji wa polepole. 在英语、斯瓦希里语和"简单说法"之间互译。

Weak · Ina udhaifu · 弱

  • Local facts: Zanzibar syllabus codes, exam formats, school dates. It will invent them confidently. Taarifa za hapa: misimbo ya mtaala wa Zanzibar, muundo wa mitihani, tarehe za shule. Itazibuni kwa ujasiri. 本地事实：桑给巴尔大纲编号、考试格式、校历——它会一本正经地编。
  • Knowing your students. It has never met the child in the back row. Kuwafahamu wanafunzi wako. Haijawahi kumwona mtoto aliyeketi mwisho. 了解你的学生。它没见过最后一排那个孩子。
  • Deciding what matters. That is the teacher's job and it stays the teacher's job. Kuamua kipi ni muhimu. Hiyo ni kazi ya mwalimu na itabaki kazi ya mwalimu. 判断什么重要。这是教师的活，永远是教师的活。
Do it live · Onyesha moja kwa moja · 现场演示
Trainer: on the projector, ask for a lesson plan with a bad prompt ("teach photosynthesis"), then the same with a good prompt from Phase 03. Show both answers side by side. Say nothing — let the difference speak. Mkufunzi: kwenye projekta, omba andalio kwa maelekezo mabaya ("fundisha usanisinuru"), kisha lile lile kwa maelekezo mazuri ya Awamu ya 03. Onyesha majibu yote mawili pamoja. Usiseme kitu — acha tofauti izungumze. 培训者：用投影，先用一句糟糕的指令（"讲光合作用"）要教案，再用第三阶段的好指令要一遍。两份答案并排放。什么都别说，让差别自己说话。

Phase 03 · The only method you must remember

Role · Task · Context · Format Jukumu · Kazi · Mazingira · Muundo 角色 · 任务 · 情境 · 格式

Four lines. Never fewer. The Context line is where Matemwe lives — 45 students, no laboratory, one projector, power that comes and goes. Fill the four boxes, then press Build. Mistari minne. Isipungue. Mstari wa Mazingira ndipo Matemwe ilipo — wanafunzi 45, hakuna maabara, projekta moja, umeme unaokatika. Jaza masanduku manne, kisha bonyeza Tengeneza. 四行，不能少。情境那一行就是马特姆韦本身——45个学生、没有实验室、一台投影、时有时无的电。填好四格，点"生成"。

Phase 04 · Hands on the keyboard

Your own lesson plan, start to finish Andalio lako mwenyewe, mwanzo hadi mwisho 你自己的教案，从头做到尾

Steps · Hatua · 步骤

  • Write your four lines about the topic in your notebook — your topic, not the example. Andika mistari yako minne kuhusu mada yako katika daftari — mada yako, si ya mfano. 就你自己的课题写下四行——不是示例的课题。
  • Send it. Read the answer to the end before you judge it. Ituma. Soma jibu hadi mwisho kabla ya kulihukumu. 发出去。读完整个回答再下判断。
  • Ask for one change, in one sentence: "The examples are foreign — use fishing, seaweed and the tide instead." Omba mabadiliko moja, kwa sentensi moja: "Mifano ni ya kigeni — tumia uvuvi, mwani na maji kujaa badala yake." 用一句话要一次修改："例子太外国了，换成捕鱼、海藻和潮汐。"
  • Repeat until you would sign your name at the bottom. Rudia hadi ukubali kuweka jina lako chini yake. 反复改，直到你愿意在末尾签自己的名字。

Trainer watches for · Mkufunzi aangalie · 培训者盯住

  • The teacher who accepts the first answer. Push them to ask for a second version. Mwalimu anayekubali jibu la kwanza. Msukume aombe toleo la pili. 谁接受了第一版答案，就推他要第二版。
  • The teacher who is stuck typing. Ask them to speak the four lines aloud, then type. Mwalimu aliyekwama kuandika. Mwambie aseme mistari minne kwa sauti, kisha aandike. 谁卡在打字上，就让他先把四行说出来，再打。
  • Hands off the keyboard. Never type for them, not once. Usiguse kibodi. Usiwaandikie, hata mara moja. 别碰键盘。一次也别替他们打。
Checkpoint · Kituo · 检查点
14:15 — three lesson plans exist, one per teacher, each with the teacher's own edits. Nobody moves on before this is true. 14:15 — maandalio matatu yapo, moja kwa kila mwalimu, kila moja likiwa na marekebisho yake mwenyewe. Hakuna anayeendelea kabla ya hili kutimia. 14:15——三份教案到位，一人一份，每份都有本人的修改。不到这一步，谁都不往下走。

Phase 05 · From paper to projector

Turn the plan into a page the students can see Geuza andalio kuwa ukurasa wanafunzi wanaoweza kuuona 把教案变成学生能看见的一页

This is the moment the work becomes real. Paste the lesson plan you just made, then send this instruction: Hapa ndipo kazi inakuwa halisi. Bandika andalio ulilotengeneza, kisha tuma maelekezo haya: 这一步，工作才真正落地。把刚做好的教案粘贴进去，然后发这条指令：

Turn this lesson plan into ONE single HTML file I can open on a laptop with no internet. Rules: no images from the web, no external links, large text readable from the back of a classroom, a title, the objectives, the five teaching steps, three questions for the students, and a summary box at the end. Give me the complete file in one block so I can copy it all at once.

Save it · Ihifadhi · 保存

  • Copy the whole block. Open Notepad / TextEdit. Paste. Nakili kila kitu. Fungua Notepad / TextEdit. Bandika. 全选复制。打开记事本／文本编辑。粘贴。
  • Save as somo1.html on the Desktop. The name must end in .html Hifadhi kama somo1.html kwenye Desktop. Jina lazima liishie na .html 存到桌面，命名 somo1.html。后缀必须是 .html
  • Double-click it. It opens in the browser. Turn off the WiFi and open it again — it still works. That file is yours forever. Bofya mara mbili. Unafunguka kwenye kivinjari. Zima WiFi ufungue tena — bado unafanya kazi. Faili hiyo ni yako milele. 双击打开，浏览器里就能看。关掉WiFi再打开，照样能用。这个文件永远是你的。

If it breaks · Ikiharibika · 出问题时

  • Page is blank: you missed the first or last line when copying. Copy again. Ukurasa mtupu: umeacha mstari wa kwanza au wa mwisho wakati wa kunakili. Nakili tena. 页面空白：复制时漏了第一行或最后一行。重新复制。
  • Code shows as text: the file was saved as .txt. Rename it, ending .html Msimbo unaonekana kama maandishi: faili imehifadhiwa kama .txt. Badilisha jina liishie .html 显示成一堆代码：文件存成了 .txt。改名，后缀改成 .html
  • Power cut: the file is already on the disk. Nothing is lost. This is why we save first. Umeme umekatika: faili tayari ipo kwenye diski. Hakuna kilichopotea. Ndiyo maana tunahifadhi kwanza. 停电：文件已经在硬盘里了，什么都没丢。所以我们先存盘。

Phase 06 · The three rules

Before it goes in front of children Kabla haijafika mbele ya watoto 在拿到孩子面前之前

  1. Check every fact you would be embarrassed to get wrong. Dates, formulas, definitions, anything with a number. If you cannot verify it, take it out. Hakiki kila ukweli ambao ungeaibika kuukosea. Tarehe, fomula, fasili, kitu chochote chenye namba. Usipoweza kuthibitisha, kiondoe. 凡是错了会丢脸的事实，一律核对。日期、公式、定义、任何带数字的东西。查不到出处，就删掉。
  2. The syllabus decides, not the machine. Open the Zanzibar syllabus next to the screen. If the AI added a topic that is not examined, cut it. Mtaala ndio unaoamua, si mashine. Fungua mtaala wa Zanzibar kando ya skrini. AI ikiongeza mada isiyoulizwa mtihani, ikate. 说了算的是大纲，不是机器。把桑给巴尔教学大纲摊在屏幕旁边。AI加了不考的内容，砍掉。
  3. No student's name, photo, illness or family situation ever goes into the AI. Describe the situation without the person: "a Form 2 student who reads slowly". Jina, picha, ugonjwa au hali ya familia ya mwanafunzi haviingii kwenye AI kamwe. Eleza hali bila mtu: "mwanafunzi wa Kidato cha Pili anayesoma polepole". 学生的姓名、照片、病情、家庭情况，永远不输入AI。只描述情况，不带人："一名阅读较慢的二年级学生"。

Phase 07 · Show and commit

Two minutes each, then a date on the board Dakika mbili kila mmoja, kisha tarehe ubaoni 每人两分钟，然后把日期写到黑板上

In the room · Darasani · 现场

  • Each teacher puts their page on the projector and teaches 60 seconds of it to the other two. Kila mwalimu aweke ukurasa wake kwenye projekta na afundishe sekunde 60 kwa wenzake wawili. 每位教师把自己的页面投上去，给另外两人讲60秒。
  • Write on the board: name, class, topic, the date you will teach it. Photograph the board. Andika ubaoni: jina, darasa, mada, tarehe utakayofundisha. Piga picha ubao. 黑板上写：姓名、班级、课题、开课日期。给黑板拍张照。

Homework · Kazi ya nyumbani · 作业

  • Teach the lesson before we meet again. Fundisha somo kabla hatujakutana tena. 下次见面前，把这节课上掉。
  • Bring three sentences: what the students did, what surprised you, what failed. Leta sentensi tatu: wanafunzi walifanya nini, kilichokushangaza, na kilichoshindikana. 带三句话来：学生做了什么、什么让你意外、什么没成。
  • Bring one thing the AI got wrong. There will be one. Finding it is the skill. Leta kitu kimoja AI ilichokosea. Kitakuwepo. Kukigundua ndio ujuzi wenyewe. 带一处AI犯的错。一定有。找得出来，才是本事。
